How DNS visibility works

VPN Router generates unique DNS lookups while binding the probe to the selected outbound tunnel, then asks the external bash.ws DNS-leak service which resolver networks observed those lookups. A matching resolver/VPN ASN is shown as “No obvious leak”; a different ASN is deliberately labelled “Potential DNS leak” because legitimate third-party DNS providers such as public encrypted resolvers can also differ from the VPN provider.
